Beacon dictionary data: attribution and licenses
================================================

dict/words.bin
--------------
dict/words.bin (plain-text TSV) contains the top 40,000 English words and their Zipf frequencies,
adapted from the word-frequency data of wordfreq (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) by Robyn Speer.
Copyright 2022 Robyn Speer.

Changes made: the English wordlist was truncated to the 40,000 most frequent words and converted to a
tab-separated table of (word, Zipf frequency x 100 as an integer) for use by Beacon's keyboard.

dict/bigrams.bin
----------------
dict/bigrams.bin (plain-text TSV): word-pair counts computed from public-domain Project Gutenberg texts
(ids 11, 84, 98, 345, 1342, 1661, 2591, 2701, 5200, 64317). Public domain.
